What your AI agent does, every day

It follows the same steps you do now, only faster, all day and night, and without typos.

Picks up new orders

Checks Amazon, Shopify, Walmart or eBay every few minutes for new orders.

Places the supplier order (PO)

Creates the purchase order on your supplier’s portal, or by email or spreadsheet. It matches the SKU to your listing and checks the address first. Anything unclear is held for you, never guessed.

Uploads tracking and confirms shipment

Picks up the tracking number from the supplier and confirms the shipment on the marketplace on time.

Keeps customers updated

Sends “your order has shipped” messages and drafts replies to “where is my order?” with live tracking.

Reports orders and profit daily

Orders in, POs placed, shipped, problems, and profit per order after supplier cost and fees.

Reports your ads weekly

Spend, sales and profit per ad across Google, Meta and Amazon Ads every Monday, with what to pause and what to scale.

Questions store owners ask

Which platforms does it work with?

Amazon Seller Central (FBM), Shopify, Walmart Marketplace, eBay and WooCommerce. On the supplier side it works with web portals, email orders, CSV/spreadsheet orders and EDI. If a person can do it in a browser, the agent can usually do it.

Will it order the wrong item?

Every order is checked before it is sent: the SKU is matched to your listing, the address is checked and the quantity is confirmed. Anything that does not match goes to a "needs you" list instead of being ordered. You can start in approve-first mode, where you press one button to send each batch.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. The agent runs on your own computer or a private server in your name, and you type your own passwords. We never store them.

How long does setup take?

Most stores are running in 1 to 3 weeks: one week to map your order flow and build, then a week or two in approve-first mode while you check its work.

What does it cost?

You get a fixed setup price after the free 15-minute audit, plus a small monthly fee for monitoring and fixes. For most stores it costs less than one month of a part-time order clerk.

What if my supplier changes their website?

Monitoring is included. If a supplier changes their site, the agent stops and alerts us instead of guessing, and we fix it.
