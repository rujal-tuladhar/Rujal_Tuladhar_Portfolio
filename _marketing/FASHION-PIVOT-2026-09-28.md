# Nova Toronto fashion pivot - first product research (2026-09-28)

Direction from Rujal: the agency is not earning. Turn Nova Toronto into a Toronto fashion/media brand.
First product: sky-blue wavy 80s-Miami style resort shirt (the look people associate with the
Vice City game). Source via Alibaba. Nova site + blog + mascot memes are the marketing platform.

## Timing hook
- Grand Theft Auto VI releases Thu Nov 19, 2026 (confirmed by Rockstar Nov 6, 2025; set in Vice City).
  52 days from today. Demand for the pastel 80s look will peak around then.
- Realistic supply timeline: sample 1-2 wks + courier 1 wk, production 2-4 wks, air freight 1-1.5 wks
  = 6-8 weeks. Stock lands mid/late November at best. So: order samples THIS WEEK, shoot content on
  the sample, open pre-orders for the Nov 19 week, ship late Nov / early Dec (holiday gift + winter
  vacation angle). Toronto summer-shirt season proper is Apr-Jul 2027 = second drop.

## IP rules (not legal advice; 1 hr with an IP lawyer + CIPO search before launch)
- Free to use: the general style. Pastel blue, palms, waves, sunsets, camp collar. Nobody owns "80s Miami".
- Do NOT use: "GTA", "Grand Theft Auto", "Vice City", "Rockstar", character names, the pink script
  logo style, game screenshots, or a copy of the in-game shirt print. Take-Two enforces its marks
  aggressively (It Takes Two, Remedy logo, mod takedowns).
- "GTA = Greater Toronto Area" pun: tempting, but on clothing in that aesthetic it invites a
  confusion claim. Do not build the brand name on it without a lawyer OK.
- Safest and most ownable: an ORIGINAL print with a Toronto twist (raccoons, streetcar with no logo,
  full skyline in the waves, lake + islands). Original art is also something competitors cannot copy.
- If using AI to draft the print, finish it by hand so it is protectable, and keep source files.

Conclusion: paid ads alone lose money on a single CA$65 shirt. Launch must be organic-first
(short video, memes, creators, markets, pre-order list); ads only for retargeting and bundles.

Sourced benchmarks: Alibaba samples US$40-150 each incl. shipping, often refunded on bulk 300+;
MOQ from 1-10 up to 100 per design. Duty on apparel from China 16-20%; GST/HST on duty-paid value.
Meta apparel: CPM US$9.23, CPC US$0.45-0.68, CPA US$20-40, median ROAS 2.18x, top quartile 4.4x.

## Legal/import checklist
- Business number + import/export (RM) account with CRA; customs broker or courier clearance.
- Textile Labelling Act: fibre content in English AND French; dealer name + postal address or a
  CA number (apply at Competition Bureau); country of origin. Have the factory sew compliant labels.
- HST registration (mandatory over CA$30k sales; voluntary earlier lets you recover border HST).
- Alibaba Trade Assurance only. Never pay outside the platform.

## Marketing plan (content first)
1. Waitlist page on novatoronto.com this week. Goal 500 emails by Nov 15. Assumed 5-10% buy = 25-50 pre-orders.
2. Short video 4-5x/week on TikTok + Reels + Shorts: shirt in Toronto locations, AI-made 80s-style
   spots (existing Nova skill), behind-the-scenes of sourcing. 15+ creatives before spending on ads.
3. Mascot: original character, monthly Toronto joke meme/animation; weekly during launch month.
4. Toronto stories blog = search + share traffic; every post carries the waitlist/shop banner.
5. Creator seeding: 15-20 shirts to Toronto micro-creators (5k-50k followers), no fee, ask for one video.
6. In person: holiday markets and pop-ups in Nov-Dec; spring/summer markets for drop 2.
7. Paid: CA$10/day retargeting site visitors and video viewers only.

Targets for drop 1: 100 shirts sold in 45 days, 60%+ from organic/waitlist.
