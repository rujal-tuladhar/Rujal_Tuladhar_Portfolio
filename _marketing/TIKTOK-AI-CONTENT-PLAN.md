# Nova Toronto - TikTok AI content plan for the first shirt (2026-09-29)

Goal: build an audience and a waitlist before the shirt exists, using AI video made in-house
(Seedance 2.5 first, Kling 3.0 second, Veo 3.1 third - Rujal's ranking).
Window: Sept 30 to Nov 19, 2026 (game launch day) = 7 weeks.

## Hard rules for every video
- Never say or show "GTA", "Grand Theft Auto", "Vice City", "Rockstar", game characters, the pink
  script logo style, or game footage. Say "80s Miami", "neon summer", "retro resort".
- The shirt print is ORIGINAL (Toronto twist). Once the real sample arrives, the shirt in every
  video must match what customers get. AI may change the scene, not the product.
- Turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label for any realistic AI video. Unlabelled realistic
  AI content can be removed or restricted.
- No real people's faces or voices without permission. Use AI characters, the mascot, or yourself.
- Music: TikTok Commercial Music Library only on a business account. No chart songs.

## Four content pillars (rotate them)
| Pillar | Share | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| A. Toronto in neon | 35% | AI video of Toronto rebuilt as an 80s Miami postcard |
| B. Mascot jokes | 25% | The Nova character, a Toronto joke, 8-15 seconds |
| C. Building the brand | 25% | Real footage: sourcing, sample unboxing, print design, prices |
| D. The shirt | 15% | Product shots, fit, try-ons, pre-order push |

Pillar C is real, not AI. Behind-the-scenes posts are what make people trust a new brand enough to buy.

## First 12 videos, with AI prompts

1. **"Toronto if it was 1986 Miami"** (A, 12s)
   Prompt: "Cinematic 1980s VHS look, Toronto waterfront at sunset reimagined as tropical Miami,
   palm trees along Queens Quay, pastel pink and sky-blue sky, neon signs reflecting on Lake
   Ontario, slow dolly forward, film grain, 4:5 vertical 9:16."
   Text on screen: "Toronto, but make it 1986." Caption: "Would you move here?"

2. **"Raccoon on vacation"** (B, 10s)
   Prompt: "Cartoon raccoon in a sky-blue wavy camp-collar shirt and sunglasses lounging on a
   Toronto Island beach chair, skyline behind, 80s synthwave palette, bouncy 2D animation, 9:16."
   Text: "Raccoons when the city finally fixes the green bin." Loop the last second.

3. **"I'm starting a clothing brand from zero"** (C, 30-45s, real)
   You to camera: why Nova, why this shirt, what it will cost. End: "Follow along. Waitlist in bio."

4. **"The TTC in 1986"** (A, 10s)
   Prompt: "Retro red streetcar with no logos driving down a palm-lined Queen Street at dusk,
   pastel neon storefronts, 1980s film look, light rain, 9:16."
   Text: "Still late, but make it aesthetic." (Do not show the TTC logo.)

5. **"Alibaba sample reveal"** (C, 20-30s, real) when the first sample lands.
   Hook in first second: "I paid $___ for this sample. Was it worth it?"

6. **"Toronto winter vs Toronto in my head"** (A+B split screen, 8s)
   Left: real slushy February street. Right AI: same street, 80s beach, palms, the shirt.

7. **"Designing the print"** (C, 20s, screen recording)
   Timelapse of the print: waves, skyline, raccoon. Ask viewers to pick between two colourways.
   Comments become the vote = free engagement.

8. **"POV: you wore this to Scarborough Town Centre"** (D+B, 10s)
   Mascot or AI character in the shirt, heads turn, text jokes. Keep it kind, not mocking the place.

9. **"The CN Tower at golden hour, 80s edition"** (A, 10s)
   Full skyline only; tower must not be the lone subject (CN Tower licensing rule).

10. **"What it costs to make one shirt"** (C, 30s, real)
    Show the real cost breakdown: factory, freight, duty, labels. Price transparency does very well.

11. **"Countdown: 30 days to launch"** (D, 8s)
    Shirt on hanger, neon light, AI background. Text: "30 days. Waitlist in bio." Repeat at 14, 7, 1.

12. **"Nov 19: the neon summer is back"** (D, 15s) launch-day video.
    Mood only, no game references. Pre-order link pinned. Post early morning Nov 19.

## Posting calendar
- Weeks 1-2 (Sept 30 - Oct 13): 1 video a day. Heavy on A and B to find what works. Videos 1-4, 6, 9.
- Weeks 3-4 (Oct 14 - 27): 1 a day, add C as the sample arrives. Videos 5, 7, 10.
- Weeks 5-7 (Oct 28 - Nov 19): 1-2 a day. Countdown posts, try-ons, pre-order push. Videos 8, 11, 12.
- Best times to test: 7-9 am, 12-1 pm, 7-10 pm Eastern. Keep what works, drop what doesn't.
- Cross-post the same files to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. No watermark.

## Production workflow per AI video (about 30-45 min each)
1. Write the hook and on-screen text first.
2. Generate 3-4 takes in Seedance; fall back to Kling for motion issues, Veo for realism.
3. Cut in CapCut: 8-15 seconds, hook in first second, captions on, loop the end.
4. Add the AI-generated label when uploading.
5. Batch: produce 5-7 videos on one day each week.
