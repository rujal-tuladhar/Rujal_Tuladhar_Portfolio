AI for Business: A Free Practical Course for Canadian Small-Business Owners

In seven short lessons you put AI to work on a few real tasks in your own business, under one page of written ground rules, and finish with the plain arithmetic that shows whether each one was worth keeping.

  • Count your week and pick the three repeated tasks that are safest and most worthwhile to hand to AI first, with a dated baseline to measure against.
  • Write a one-page staff AI policy with a never-enter list, a named privacy contact and a short table of approved tools.
  • Build a one-page facts sheet, then use it for saved customer replies, review responses and an FAQ you can stand behind.
  • Turn one real job into a week of posts, an email and ad lines, and check them against Canada's advertising and anti-spam rules.
  • Draft quotes, payment reminders and written procedures with AI while a calculator and a named person check every figure.
  • Decide with your own missed-call numbers whether an AI receptionist deserves a trial, then score every AI use as keep, fix or drop.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All seven lessons are free, with no payment details asked for. The course is published by Nova Toronto, a Toronto studio that sells AI set-up services, and the final lesson says so plainly. You are never required to buy anything.

Do I need to pay for any AI tool?

No. The exercises can be done on the free plan of an AI assistant you already use, and none of them asks you to enter customer personal information. Lesson 2 explains why a business account is the safer choice for regular business work and shows you how to work out whether a paid plan would earn its cost. That decision stays with you.

How long does it take?

Each lesson is about 15 minutes to read, with an exercise of roughly 10 to 15 minutes. A few exercises have follow-up steps to finish later in the week, such as getting staff to initial a policy or checking a provider's pricing page. You can go at your own pace.

Will you spam me?

No. You will get this course by email and occasional practical tips from Nova Toronto. You can stop the emails at any time by replying STOP or by emailing novatoronto.ca@gmail.com.

Do I need technical skills or a computer?

No technical skills are needed, only the ability to use an AI chat assistant and write a clear prompt. If that is new to you, start with the free AI for Beginners course at novatoronto.com/ai-course/. Most exercises work on a phone with a note and a calculator. One or two optional set-up steps, such as turning on Gmail templates, need a computer.
