About the book

Incontinence Care at Home is the handbook nobody hands you at the pharmacy: which of the seven product types fits which situation, how to measure and fit them, the two-minute change routine that protects skin, the night setup that lets everyone sleep, bowel incontinence, outings and travel, equipment, what it costs and what may be covered, and who to call. It is written by Rujal Tuladhar, who supplies incontinence products to thousands of families and has heard every question. Find it on Amazon.

This book and these checklists are practical information, not medical advice. New or worsening incontinence always deserves a conversation with a doctor or nurse.
