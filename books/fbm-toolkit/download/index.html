Your FBM Launch Toolkit is ready

One Excel workbook, seven tabs. Open the Settings tab first and enter your supplier's drop-ship fee, shipping cost and your profit floor. Every other tab reads from it.

Three things that make the toolkit work

  1. Never type a fee twice. The Settings tab is the only place fees live. If your supplier changes its rate card, change it there once.
  2. Dedup by ASIN, never by product. The Research Tracker has a column for "already live on this ASIN". Use it exactly as Chapter 5 describes.
  3. HOLD is a valid answer. On the Repricing Audit, a HOLD row is a listing that earns nothing today and costs nothing to keep. Leave it.
