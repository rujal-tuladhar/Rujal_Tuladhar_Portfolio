What is in the workbook

1

Settings

Referral fee percentage, drop-ship fee under and over $100, outbound shipping, broken-case self-ship cost and your minimum profit per unit. Change these first.
2

Profit Calculator

Supplier price, pack sizes and the competitor's lowest total in; your price split, Amazon fee, drop-ship fee, shipping and profit out, with PASS or FAIL on every row.
3

Research Tracker

The pipeline log: brand, gating status, UPC, ASINs found, seller count, FBA present, already live, decision.
4

Repricing Audit

The fortnightly Featured Offer audit. Enter the featured total and the supplier price; the sheet tells you MATCH or HOLD.
5

Order Log

Amazon order ID to PO (last seven digits) automatically, supplier reference, carrier, tracking, confirmed date and a NOT ORDERED flag.
6

Monthly Report

Gross, Amazon fees, net deposited, supplier cost, overhead, business profit and net margin, six months side by side.
7

30-Day Plan

Every task from the launch chapter, by week and day, with the chapter to re-read and a done column.

About the book

Sell on Amazon Canada Without Touching Inventory is the operating manual for a merchant-fulfilled (FBM) Amazon.ca store supplied by a Canadian distributor who ships straight to your customers under your name. It is written by Rujal Tuladhar, who runs one: about four hundred live listings, an order loop that runs on a schedule, and real numbers, including the unflattering ones. Find it on Amazon.ca.
