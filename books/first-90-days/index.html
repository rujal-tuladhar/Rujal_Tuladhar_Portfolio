About the book

Your First 90 Days in Ontario is the order of operations for a new life here: the first-week sequence, your SIN the same day, OHIP with no waiting period, a bank account and a credit score from zero, renting without getting cheated, the driver's licence rules, finding work, the benefits you are owed, school and child care, the free settlement services most newcomers never use, your first winter, the scams, and a week-by-week plan. Written by Rujal Tuladhar, who immigrated to Toronto and built businesses here, and checked against official sources. Find it on Amazon.ca.

Practical information, not legal or immigration advice. For immigration matters use only a lawyer or a licensed consultant.
