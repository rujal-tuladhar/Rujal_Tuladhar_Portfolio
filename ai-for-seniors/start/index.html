AI for Seniors: Everyday Help and Staying Safe, in 7 Short Lessons

Your lessons

Here are your seven lessons, each about 12 to 15 minutes long with a short practice exercise for your phone or tablet. Start with Lesson 1 and go at your own pace, or go straight to Lesson 6 if you are worried about a call or message right now.

1

Your First Conversation With an AI Helper

Install a genuine free AI chat app, ask one question and one follow-up, and name the three ground rules that apply in every lesson.
15 min 2

Talk Instead of Typing, and Ask Again Until It Is Clear

Use your voice, larger text and the read-aloud button to hold a comfortable conversation, and steer an answer with short spoken follow-ups.
12 min 3

Writing Messages and Cards to Family and Friends

Draft a personal message or card with AI, adjust it until it sounds like you, and keep private details out of the chat.
12 min 4

Making Sense of a Confusing Bill or Letter

Get a plain-language explanation of a confusing bill or letter after leaving out your personal details, and decide which real person or office to confirm it with.
15 min 5

Planning Meals, Outings and Hobbies

Plan a few days of meals, a small outing or a hobby project with AI, and confirm the details that matter before relying on them.
12 min 6

AI Scam Calls and Messages: Pause, Hang Up, Call Back

Recognize the warning signs of AI voice-cloning "grandparent" calls and fake messages, and respond with one routine: pause, hang up, call back on a number you already have.
15 min 7

Fake Pictures, Reporting Fraud and Your Safety Card

Question pictures and videos that may be fake, know where Canadians report fraud, and make a one-page safety card to keep by the phone.
