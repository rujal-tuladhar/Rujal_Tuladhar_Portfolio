AI for Seniors: Everyday Help and Staying Safe, in 7 Short Lessons

A free, calm course that shows you how to use an AI chat app on your phone or tablet for everyday things, and how to protect yourself from AI-powered scams.

  • Install a genuine free AI chat app from your official app store and have your first conversation with it.
  • Talk instead of typing, make the text larger, and ask again until an answer is clear.
  • Write a card or message to family and friends that still sounds like you.
  • Turn a confusing bill or letter into plain words, after covering up your personal details.
  • Plan a few suppers, a small outing or a hobby project, and confirm the details that matter.
  • Use one routine for suspicious calls and messages (pause, hang up, call back) and know where Canadians report fraud.

Who this course is for

  • Adults of about 60 and older who are curious about AI, or a little nervous about it.
  • People who use a phone or tablet more than a computer.
  • Anyone who wants help with everyday life, without putting privacy or savings at risk.
  • Family members who would like a patient, step-by-step course to share with a parent or grandparent.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All seven lessons are free. The course never asks for a card number, banking details or any payment.

Do I need to pay for any AI tool?

No. Every exercise works on the free plan of one AI chat app. Lesson 1 shows you how to install a genuine one from your official app store. You may see an offer for a paid plan inside the app, and you can say no.

How long does it take?

Each lesson takes about 12 to 15 minutes, including a short practice exercise. All seven come to roughly an hour and a half. Go at your own pace and repeat any lesson as often as you like.

Will you spam me?

No. You will get this course by email and occasional practical tips from Nova Toronto. You can stop the emails at any time by replying STOP or writing to novatoronto.ca@gmail.com. We do not sell or share your email address.

Do I need technical skills or a computer?

No. You need a phone or tablet and an email address. Every technical word is explained the first time it appears, and each step is written out in plain language. You do not need a computer or a printer.
